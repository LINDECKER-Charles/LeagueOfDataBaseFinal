3. Audience measurement in detail

Traffic is measured without cookies, by tools the publisher hosts on the site's own server. No third-party tool (Google Analytics or similar) is used, no data is sold or shared with anyone, and the statistics are accessible to the publisher only. Two measures currently run side by side, while we check that the new one counts like the old one, which will then be removed.

Umami

For every page you open, the site itself sends, from your browser and to its own address, a record to Umami, an open-source audience measurement tool the publisher hosts on this server. No Umami script is ever loaded in the pages. A record contains:

  • the page's address, reduced before it is sent: the path of the catalogue's lists and detail pages, with the Data Dragon language and version; for any other page, its template only (for example /en/u/:username or /b/:token), without any pseudonym, token or identifier. Never a list's searches and filters, and nothing at all from the account and administration pages;
  • the page's title, for the lists and detail pages only;
  • on the first page of a visit, the origin of the site that brought you here (for example https://www.google.com), without any path or parameter;
  • your browser's language, your screen size, the platform (website), the site's language and the version viewed;
  • three events without content: a page not found or failing, a search started in a list (never its text), a build's link copied.

Like any server, Umami also receives your IP address and your User-Agent. It derives from them the browser, the operating system, the device type, the country, the region and the city, and a hashed session identifier whose key changes every month: it counts visitors without recognising them from one month to the next, and keeps neither the IP address nor the User-Agent. No account identifier is ever sent to it. These records are kept for 25 months at most.

In-house measure

The server also counts the views of the public pages (home page, lists and detail pages for champions, items, runes and summoner spells). For each page view it records the timestamp (UTC), the page (route and path), the resource and entity displayed, the HTTP status, the Data Dragon version and language and the interface language; the browser, operating system, device type and a "bot" flag, derived from the User-Agent; the referring site (host and category: direct, internal, search engine, social network, other); a pseudonymous visitor identifier, a truncated HMAC-SHA256 fingerprint of the IP address and the User-Agent computed with a key known only to the server; and, when a GeoLite2 geolocation database is installed on the server, the country, resolved locally, with no request to any external service.

The IP address and the User-Agent serve these computations at the time of the visit and are not stored; those of visits before 11 October 2026 are erased 30 days after the visit. Events are deleted after 13 months. The daily aggregates drawn from them (counters, and the fingerprints of the day's visitors) contain neither IP addresses nor User-Agent strings and are kept without a time limit.

Hosting access log

The front server that receives every request sent to the site logs each of them: IP address, User-Agent, requested address (path and parameters), page the request comes from (referrer), response status and the city derived from the IP address. This log serves security and operations (spotting abuse, diagnosing an outage), on the basis of legitimate interest; it is kept for 90 days and is accessible to the publisher only.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the data held about you;
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (email, summoner name…);
  • Erasure — the "Delete my account" button on the profile page immediately deletes your account and all your builds; you may also request erasure by email;
  • Portability — receive the data you provided in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including audience measurement;
  • Restriction — request the temporary freezing of a processing activity.

To exercise these rights: {{ info().dpoEmail }} or {{ info().publisherEmail }}. You will receive an answer within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr.
